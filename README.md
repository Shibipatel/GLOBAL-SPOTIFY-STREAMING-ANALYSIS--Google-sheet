# 🎵 Global Spotify Streaming Analysis Dashboard

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive *Global Spotify Streaming Analysis Dashboard, designed using **Google Sheet*. 
The dashboard provides visual insights into global music streaming trends across 500 top artists, analyzing lead vs. feature stream dynamics, solo vs. collaborative performance, genre breakdown, debut era longevity, gender representation, and country-level streaming reach.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard includes the following key visualizations:

1. *Executive KPI Cards*
   - *Total Artists:* 500
   - *Total Streams:* 8,408,805.1 M (8.4+ Trillion streams)
   - *Avg. Stream per Artist:* 16,817.6 M

2. *Top 10 Artists by Total Stream*
   - Horizontal bar chart tracking the highest streamed artists led by *Drake (137,492.1 M), **Taylor Swift (127,881.0 M), and **Bad Bunny (125,099.8 M)*, followed by The Weeknd, Justin Bieber, Ariana Grande, Travis Scott, Kanye West, Ed Sheeran, and Eminem.

3. *Stream-Type Analysis (Lead Streams vs. Feature Streams)*
   - 100% stacked bar chart analyzing each top artist's stream composition (e.g., Taylor Swift with 99% Lead vs. 1% Feature; Drake with 69% Lead vs. 31% Feature).

4. *Solo vs. Collaborative Streams*
   - Comparative horizontal bar chart evaluating the split between solo releases and collaborative tracks for the top performers.

5. *Solo vs. Group Distribution*
   - Donut chart highlighting the market dominance of *Solo artists (79.9%)* versus *Groups / Bands (20.1%)*.

6. *Total Streams by Debut Year Group*
   - Column chart mapping stream volume across artist debut eras: *Before 2000 (1,528.77 B), **2000–2009 (2,717.35 B), **2010–2014 (2,577.93 B), **2015–2019 (1,424.48 B), and **2020+ (160.41 B)*.

7. *Gender-Based Streaming*
   - Bar chart showing gender share of streams: *Male (77%), **Female (19%), and **Mixed/Groups (4%)*.

8. *Total Stream by Genre*
   - Ranked horizontal bar chart identifying *Hip-Hop* and *Pop* as the dominant streaming genres, followed by Reggaeton, Rock, R&B, and Latin.

9. *Total Streams by Country & World Map*
   - Geographic choropleth map paired with a ranked table highlighting top markets led by the *United States (3,706,444.0 M), **United Kingdom (652,177.6 M), and **Canada (496,760.1 M)*.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reveals the following key insights:

1. *Top Tier Dominance:* The top 3 artists (*Drake, Taylor Swift, and Bad Bunny) each cross **125 Billion streams*, dominating global streaming share.
2. *Catalog Longevity:* Artists who debuted between *2000 and 2014* account for the vast majority of listening volume (*over 5.2 Trillion streams combined*), showing that established catalogs drive long-term platform engagement.
3. *Release Strategy Divergence:* *Taylor Swift* drives virtually all her listening through original catalog ownership (*99% lead streams), while artists like **Drake (31% features)* and *Bad Bunny (35% features)* leverage strategic collaborations to scale audience reach.
4. *Solo Artists Lead:* Solo acts account for *~80%* of total platform streaming volume compared to only ~20% from musical groups/bands.
5. *Top Genres & Markets:* *Hip-Hop and Pop* lead the streaming landscape globally, with the *United States* standing as the largest stream contributor by a wide margin (3.7T+ streams).

## 🎯 Final Conclusion & Recommendations
* *Prioritize Catalog Acquisition for 2000–2014 Artists:* Music labels and streaming curators should focus on catalog preservation and remaster campaigns for artists from the 2000–2014 debut cohorts, as they command the strongest recurring replay value.
* *Leverage Strategic Features for Global Growth:* Emerging and solo artists looking to break into top markets (US, UK, Latin America) should prioritize cross-genre and cross-language collaborations, which proven leaders (Drake, Bad Bunny) successfully utilize to capture multi-market share.
